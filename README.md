# Sahal Travel Agency Management System ✈️🌍
**Nidaamka Casriga ah ee Maamulka Wakaaladaha Safarka & Dalxiiska**

Nidaamkan waxaa si gaar ah loogu dhisay wakaaladaha safarka (Travel Agencies) si ay u maamulaan boosaska rakaabka, lacagaha, xirmooyinka dalxiiska, iyo macaamiisha.

---

## 🌟 Qeybaha Muhiimka ah ee Nidaamka (Key Features):

1. **Dashboard & Dulmar Guud (Analytics & KPIs):**
   - **Dakhliga Guud (Total Revenue):** Muujinta lacagaha dhabta ah ee soo xarooday.
   - **Kharashaadka (Total Expenses):** Kharashaadka diyaaradaha, kirada xafiiska, mushaharka shaqaalaha, iwm.
   - **Macaashka Saafiga ah (Net Profit):** Faaiidada dhabta ah ee u soo hartay shirkadda.
   - **Lacagaha Dhiman / Daymaha (Pending Balances):** Lacagta ay macaamiishu ku leeyihiin safarradooda.
   - **Safarrada Guud & Macaamiisha:** Tirada tikidhada la gooyay iyo macmiil kasta.
   - **Shaxanno Xisaabeed (Charts & Analytics):**
     * Isbarbardhigga bilaha ee Dakhliga & Kharashka (Chart.js Bar Chart).
     * Qeybaha Adeegyada (Dalxiis, Cumro, Nasiino, Safari, Duulimaad).
     * Hababka lacag bixinta (EVC Plus, ZAAD Service, Sahal, Kaash, Kaarka Bangiga).

2. **Maamulka Boosaska & Tikidhada (Bookings & Boarding Passes):**
   - Diiwaangelinta macmiil cusub: Magaca, taleefanka, baasaboorka, goobta, taariikhda bixitaanka & soo laabashada, tirada dadka, qiimaha guud, iyo lacagta horumarka loo bixiyay.
   - **Xisaabinta tooska ah:** Nidaamku wuxuu si toos ah u xisaabinayaa lacagta dhiman.
   - **Daabacaadda Tikidhka (E-Ticket / Boarding Pass):** Tikidh diyaaradeed oo qurux badan oo leh barcode, xogta duulimaadka, xisaabta, iyo shaabadda rasmiga ah oo diyaar u ah daabacaad (Print).
   - Ku daridda lacag-bixin dheeraad ah (Partial Payment Settlement).

3. **Goobaha & Xirmooyinka Dalxiiska (Destinations & Photo Gallery):**
   - Sawirro tayo sare leh oo ku saabsan goobaha caanka ah (Istanbul, Makkah & Madinah Cumro, Dubai, Zanzibar, Cairo, Nairobi Safari).
   - **Sawir Soo Gelis (Image Upload):** Waxaad sawirro cusub kaga soo gelin kartaa kumbuyuutarkaaga (Computer File Upload) ama waxaad gelin kartaa Link-ga sawirka (Image URL).
   - Muujinta muddada safarka, qiimaha qofkiiba, iyo adeegyada ku jira (Tikidh, Hoteel, Fiiso, iwm).

4. **Maamulka Lacagaha & Xisaabaadka (Financial Ledger & Accounting):**
   - Diiwaangelin buuxda oo Dakhli (Income) iyo Kharash (Expense).
   - Taageero dhameystiran oo loogu talagalay lacagaha Soomaaliya: **EVC Plus, ZAAD Service, Sahal, Kaash, iyo Bank Transfer**.
   - Soo dejinta xogta xisaabta oo fayl **Excel / CSV** ah hal gujin ku soo degaya (`btnExportFinanceCSV`).

5. **Macaamiisha & WhatsApp Toos ah (Client Directory & WhatsApp):**
   - Diiwaanka macmiil kasta, tirada safarrada uu galay, wadarta lacagta uu bixiyay, iyo deynta ku dhiman.
   - Badhan toos ah oo **WhatsApp** lagu furayo si macmiilka fariin loogu diro.

3. Wax rakibaad ah (Install) ama internet adag uma baahna, xogtiisuna waxay ku keydsan tahay kumbuyuutarkaaga (LocalStorage).
